Questions

Common questions.

Who can help with marketing for a small business in Ely?

Lewis Phillips is a marketing consultant based in Ely who helps small businesses across Ely and Cambridgeshire. That's me: I plan the marketing and do the work, from websites, local search and Google Ads to the tracking and systems behind them.

What does a marketing consultant do for a small business?

A marketing consultant helps a small business decide what to do about its marketing, then helps get it done. I look at the full picture, decide what needs attention first and do the work: websites, advertising, tracking and systems.

Should I use a marketing consultant or an agency?

A consultant suits a business that wants to work directly with one senior person; an agency gives you a team. With Phillips. you work with me, Lewis Phillips, throughout: instead of a marketing hire, or alongside the people you already have.

Which areas do you cover?

Phillips. works with businesses in Ely, Soham, Littleport, Cambridge, Newmarket and the villages between, and across Cambridgeshire. Consultancy for larger and international companies isn't limited by area.

How much does it cost?

Websites start at £995, with technical maintenance and security at £45 a month. See website pricing for what's included. I charge from £500 a month to manage Google Ads. My free Google Ads Check shows your monthly management fee and a priced list of one-off fixes.

Can you help my business show up in ChatGPT and Google's AI answers?

Yes, I can help your business show up in ChatGPT and Google's AI answers. I make sure your website answers customers' questions clearly, your details match across listings and review sites, and your site uses structured data. On a monthly plan, I check about 10 questions your customers would ask across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot and Google's AI Overviews. Each month, I report whether your business was named, which sources the tools used and what to fix. There's no guarantee your business will appear.

Is there a free Google Ads or website check?

Yes. The Google Ads Check reviews your account settings and the last 30 days of results. The Website Check reviews one page, including how quickly it loads and how easy it is to get in touch. Both show what's working and what to fix first. The checks run without making changes. Both are on the Free checks page.
