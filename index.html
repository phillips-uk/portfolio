Common questions.

Who can help with marketing for a small business in Ely?

Lewis Phillips is a marketing consultant based in Ely who helps small businesses across Ely and Cambridgeshire. That's me: I plan the marketing and do the work, from websites, local search and Google Ads to the tracking and systems behind them.

What does a marketing consultant do for a small business?

A marketing consultant helps a small business decide what to do about its marketing, then helps get it done. I look at the full picture, decide what needs attention first and do the work: websites, advertising, tracking and systems.

Should I use a marketing consultant or an agency?

A consultant suits a business that wants to work directly with one senior person; an agency gives you a team. With Phillips. you work with me, Lewis Phillips, throughout: instead of a marketing hire, or alongside the people you already have.

Which areas do you cover?

Phillips. works with businesses in Ely, Soham, Littleport, Cambridge, Newmarket and the villages between, and across Cambridgeshire. Consultancy for larger and international companies isn't limited by area.
