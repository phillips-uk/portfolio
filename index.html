01

Tracking & Measurement Audits

Automates checks across GTM, Google Ads, GA4, Enhanced Conversions and Consent Mode v2, then produces a prioritised fix plan instead of a generic issue list. Built to catch tracking problems before they distort reporting or bidding signals.

02

Weekly Performance Workflow

Pulls Google Ads performance data, runs search term checks, highlights wasted spend and produces a weekly account deep-dive with clear action items. Reports are written into Obsidian, exported where needed, and pushed into Monday.com so analysis turns into follow-through.

03

Feed & SEO Optimisation

Audits Merchant Center and Shopify product data, then proposes improvements to Shopping titles, product types, SEO titles and meta descriptions. The workflow keeps human review in place before anything is applied, so automation speeds up the work without blindly changing live accounts.

Automation Toolkit

Practical automation tools built to reduce manual analysis, catch problems earlier and turn performance data into clear next actions.

  • Tracking Audit Checks GTM, Google Ads, GA4, Enhanced Conversions and Consent Mode v2, then produces a prioritised fix plan. Built to catch tracking issues before they damage reporting, attribution or Smart Bidding signals.
  • Weekly Performance Review Pulls Google Ads data, reviews search terms, highlights wasted spend and generates a weekly campaign deep-dive with action items. Turns account review work into a repeatable workflow instead of a manual Monday slog.
  • Feed Health Audits Merchant Center and Shopify product data, checking approval rates, product fields and Shopping title quality. Suggests feed improvements for review before approved changes are applied via Shopify.
  • SEO Audit Reviews Shopify title tags, meta descriptions, image alt text, thin content and orphan products, then combines this with Core Web Vitals and Search Console data to prioritise the fixes most likely to matter.
  • Search Console Review Analyses ranking movement each week, highlighting quick wins, CTR gaps, declining queries and new top-20 entries so SEO work is based on movement and opportunity, not guesswork.
