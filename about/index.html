My story

From a designer marketplace to eight markets.

I started in 2015 by founding The Pommier, a Shopify marketplace for independent jewellery and accessories designers. It grew to more than 100 designers from over 25 countries, with a shop in Covent Garden and pop-ups at Boxpark Shoreditch. Two of its designers, Myia Bonner and Lee Renée, became clients.

At Angi I ran paid media for homeowner and tradesperson marketplaces in eight markets, including MyBuilder in the UK and HomeStars in Canada. I designed the bidding architecture across paid search, paid social and display, ran a structured testing programme, and interviewed and trained performance marketing teams across Europe.

Today I manage paid acquisition for Go City, one of the world's leading destination experience brands, and I consult through Phillips. I also built and run The Director's Office, Before You Pay and Search Desk.

For clients, I plan the marketing and then do the work: the ads, the website, the tracking and the systems. I can do it instead of a full-time hire, or alongside the people already doing your marketing.
