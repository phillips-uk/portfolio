My story

Founder, small business, corporate, global.

It started with my own money. I taught myself marketing by building The Pommier, a Shopify marketplace for independent jewellery and accessories designers. Self-funded, it grew from a website into a business selling £900k a year, with two retail outlets in central London and more than 240 designers from over 25 countries. I ran the whole marketing lifecycle: paid ads, PR, SEO and affiliates.

Read more

Then small businesses. Two of its designers, Lee Renée and Myia Bonner, became clients. I built the Lee Renée and Myia Bonner websites and set SEO strategies focused on specific product searches. Visits and sales from searches that don't include their brand names are still growing across Google Search, Shopping, Maps and AI Overviews.

Then scale. I joined MyBuilder, later acquired by Angi, and scaled its UK paid media into eight figures a year, then further as Angi brought in businesses across Europe and North America. I ran eight markets, designed the bidding architecture and trained teams across Europe. It's where the instincts I'd taught myself became a method.

Now global. At Go City I work on how people discover and book attractions across many destinations, languages and currencies. Alongside that I consult through Phillips., and I built and run The Director's Office, Before You Pay, Index Point and Search Desk.

So I speak more than one business language: a founder's, spending their own money; a small business owner's, short on time; a startup's, moving fast; and a corporate team's, answering to a board. For clients, I plan the marketing and then do the work, instead of a full-time hire or alongside your team.
