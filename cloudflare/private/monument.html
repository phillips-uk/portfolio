FAQs

Before you get in touch

What is asset-based lending?

A facility secured on the assets a business already has: what its customers owe, its inventory, its plant and machinery and sometimes its property. Because it is tied to those assets rather than to past profits, the amount available can rise as the business grows.

How much could we borrow?

It depends on the assets and how lenders value them. Receivables usually support the highest advance, with inventory, plant and property at lower rates. The calculator gives an indicative figure, and the first conversation gives a clearer one.

What does trade finance cover?

Funding across the trade cycle: paying suppliers, letters of credit, goods in transit and stock, until the customer pays. It suits importers, exporters and distributors whose cash is tied up in goods.

Can asset-based lending fund an acquisition or a refinance?

Yes. Asset-based facilities are often used to fund buy-and-build acquisitions and management buyouts, and to move a business to a lender that offers more headroom.

What size of business do you work with?

Privately owned companies. Recent facilities have ranged from £2m to £20m. If your requirement is smaller or larger, start an enquiry and we will tell you plainly whether we can help.

Which lenders do you work with?

High street banks and independent lenders, chosen to fit the facility and the business.

How are fees charged?

How fees work goes here, for example whether there is a fee on completion and when it is paid.

Do you only work in London and Cambridgeshire?

No. We work from London and Cambridgeshire, and recent facilities include businesses in the UK, Sweden, Latvia, Nigeria and the UAE.
