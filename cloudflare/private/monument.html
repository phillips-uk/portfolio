Questions finance directors ask

What is asset-based lending?

It is borrowing secured against assets such as debtors, stock, plant and machinery, or property. The amount available can rise if the value of eligible assets increases and the facility terms allow it.

What does trade finance cover?

It can cover supplier payments, goods in transit and stock held before customers pay. The product depends on where the company needs funding in the trade cycle.

Can asset-based lending fund an acquisition or a refinance?

An asset-based facility can help fund an acquisition or replace existing borrowing. The amount available depends on the eligible assets and the lender’s terms.

Which lenders does Ben work with?

Ben works with high street banks and independent lenders.
