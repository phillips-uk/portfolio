Questions about working with Ben

Which lenders does Ben work with?

Ben Boateng works with high street banks and independent lenders. Each lender decides whether it can fund the company and sets its own terms, including what the company can draw and what security it needs.

Does Ben Boateng work with companies outside London and Cambridgeshire?

Yes. Ben Boateng works from London and Cambridgeshire, and the facilities he has arranged include businesses in the UK, Sweden, Latvia, Nigeria and the UAE.
