      How a week works
      1. Friday: the drafting task writes the issue from the platforms' own announcements and uploads it here. At 16:00 you get an email saying it's ready (or that it's missing).
      2. Preview the page and the email, send yourself a test, then press Approve. Changes: ask a Claude session to edit the draft, then approve again.
      3. Monday 07:00: it goes to everyone who confirmed, and the page goes live. You get a note when it's sent, or if it wasn't approved.

      Hourly rate

      £–
      How this works

      Hourly rate is the day rate divided by the hours. A report adds the set-up once to whatever is ticked, then prices each job on its time. Prices round to the nearest pound. The rate card page shows the day rate in its own page, so update it there too if it changes.

      Google Ads monthly

      Not set
      How this works

      Monthly fee = the account’s estimated hours a month × the hourly rate, rounded, and never less than the minimum. Hours come from the campaigns that spent in the last 30 days and the 30-day spend. An empty minimum hides the monthly option.

      Time per fix

      set-up + minutes per item, from the account's own counts
      Fix typeWhat counts as an itemBy hand: set-up (min)By hand: per item (min)With AI: set-up (min)With AI: per item (min)
      How this works

      Reports price each job on its AI-assisted time. Leave both AI boxes empty for a job that can't be done quickly with AI: it keeps the time by hand. The hand times are estimates made at build on 16 Nov 2026 and the AI times on 19 Nov 2026; tune both against real jobs.

      Free-use limits

      How this works

      Explorer access allows 2,880 Google Ads operations a day and a check uses about 25 to 60, so keep the daily ceiling under about 48 until Basic access is approved.
