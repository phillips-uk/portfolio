Questions finance directors ask

Which payment terms should a commodity trader compare?

Compare the date the supplier can claim payment with the date the customer is due to pay. Include transit and storage where these affect the gap.

Can receivables finance cover the period after delivery?

Eligible unpaid invoices may support borrowing after delivery. The lender decides which invoices qualify and how much it will advance.

Trade finance for commodity trading companies: funding explained

The gap between the purchase and the sale

A trader can owe the supplier before the buyer pays. Start by comparing the payment dates in the purchase and sale contracts. Check what evidence each party must provide before payment is due. Build in time for transport and any inspection required under the contracts. Where more than one shipment is due to be paid for before a buyer settles, show the combined cash requirement on those dates.

Checking exposure before drawdown

Put the purchase price, expected sale proceeds and each payment currency into the cash flow forecast. If the sale price is still to be fixed, show how a change could affect the cash available to repay the facility. Check which party bears the cost of transport, insurance and storage. A delay can change both the cost of the trade and how long the company needs to borrow.

Documents and receivables

The lender may ask for the purchase and sale contracts, transport documents and details of stock held in storage. A letter of credit specifies which documents the supplier must present for payment under the credit. Where the company also needs funding after delivery, check when it can issue the invoice and whether the debtor and invoice meet the lender’s eligibility terms. The invoice due date and the actual receipt date can differ.

Facilities in fertiliser and oil and gas

Ben arranged an £8m trade finance facility for a Latvian fertiliser business and a £5m trade and receivables facility for a Nigerian oil and gas importer. For another transaction, show when funds need to go out, what would trigger repayment and how those dates could change if delivery or payment slips.
