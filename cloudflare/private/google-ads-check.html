Invite my manager account, read only

  1. In Google Ads, open Admin, then Access and security, then Managers.
  2. Press the blue + button and enter 495-618-4074.
  3. If Google asks for an access level, choose Read only. Then send the invitation.

The check accepts the invitation, reads the account and removes the link straight away. Nothing in your account is changed.

        What it checks

        The account checks I'd run by hand.

        Set-up and structure as well as recent results, so you see what's built right and what's costing you. Fixed written rules, so the same account always gets the same answer.

        • Conversion trackingWhether enquiries and sales are recorded, whether small actions are counted as if they were sales, and whether any sale or lead may be counted twice.
        • Campaign settingsLocation targeting, networks, ad rotation and the settings that quietly waste budget.
        • Search termsSearches that spend and never convert, and the negatives that would stop them.

        • Ads and assetsAd groups without enough ads, and missing sitelinks, callouts and other assets.
        • Bidding and budgetsWhether the bid strategy suits the conversions you have, and budgets that run out.

        Questions

        Questions about the check.

        Is this the same as a Google Ads audit?

        Yes, a free one. It covers the main areas of a Google Ads audit: conversion tracking, settings, search terms, keywords, ads, bidding, structure, and Shopping and Performance Max. The report shows what's working, what needs attention and where to start.

        How long does it take, and do I need to sign up?

        It takes a few minutes and there is nothing to sign up for. Sign in with Google, or invite my manager account read only from inside Google Ads.

        Is the Google Ads Check free?

        Yes, for small and medium businesses: one free check per Google Ads account every 30 days. Agencies checking clients' accounts can ask me about a paid audit.

        Can you change anything in my account?

        The check only reads. You invite my manager account from inside Google Ads, the check reads the account, and the link is removed as soon as it finishes. Nothing in your account changes unless you buy a fix and approve the exact list of changes first.

        What does it look at?

        Conversion tracking, campaign settings such as location targeting and networks, search terms and negatives, keywords and Quality Score, ads and assets, bidding and budgets, and Shopping and Performance Max where you run them. It also checks that your most used landing pages load, summarises the changes Google Ads reports for the last 28 days and shows up to 13 months of results.

        What happens to my data?

        The report is saved for 30 days so its link works, then deleted. I keep a scrambled version of the account ID to allow one free check every 30 days.

        Does it use AI?

        No, on purpose. Every result comes from fixed, written rules, the checks I'd run by hand. The same data always gets the same answer, every figure can be traced back to your account, and your account data never goes to an AI company.

        Can I check my account myself?

        Yes. My Google Ads audit checklist takes you through the same checks in the same order and shows you what to fix first.

        How is it different from Search Desk?

        The check looks at how the account is set up and built, once. Search Desk watches performance every day and shows what changed and what needs fixing.
