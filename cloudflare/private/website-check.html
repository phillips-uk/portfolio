      Questions about the check.

      Is this a website audit?

      Yes, a free one. It reads the page you enter and up to 24 more from the same site. It looks at speed and what Google says to fix, the phone layout, accessibility, what the page says and how to get in touch, search basics, link previews, technical set-up, the UK legal basics and whether AI assistants can read the page.

      How long does it take, and do I need an account?

      About a minute, and there is no account or sign-in. Enter the address of a public page on any UK website.

      Is the Website Check free?

      Yes. It is free for small and medium businesses and needs no sign-in. You can run up to five checks a day.

      What does it look at?

      The public page you enter and up to 24 more from the same site: broken links and site-wide search basics, the page’s speed on a phone and a computer, how it shows on a phone, accessibility, whether it says what you do and how to get in touch, your contact details, page titles, canonical tag and structured data, link previews, https, security headers and missing pages, robots.txt and sitemap, whether AI assistants can read it, Google Analytics or Tag Manager, a privacy policy and cookie banner, company details, and reviews.

      Do you change anything on my website?

      No. The check only reads pages anyone can see. It never signs in and never changes anything.

      What happens to the address I enter?

      The report is saved for 30 days so its link works, then deleted. Your IP address is not stored; a scrambled version is used only to count checks per day.

      Does it use AI?

      No. Every result comes from fixed, written rules. Only Google's speed scores can vary a little between runs.

      How many pages does it check?

      The page you enter and up to 24 more: the pages your menu links to first, then pages from your sitemap. Speed is measured on the page you enter. It respects your robots.txt and reads a few pages at a time.

      Tell me about the business and what you’d like the website to do. If you ran a check, the report link is added for you. I’ll reply by email.
