What it checks

The things that turn a visit into an enquiry.

The same checks I run when someone asks me to look at their website, written as fixed rules so the same page always gets the same answer.

  • SpeedGoogle's PageSpeed test on a phone and a computer, and real visitors' Core Web Vitals when Google has them.
  • PhonesThe page fits the screen, text is readable and buttons are easy to tap.
  • First impressionA clear heading that says what you do and where, and a way to get in touch near the top.
  • Contact detailsPhone, email and address, written the same everywhere, and a link to your Google Business Profile.
  • Search basicsPage title and description, one main heading and LocalBusiness structured data.
  • Technicalhttps, robots.txt and a sitemap.
  • AI searchWhether ChatGPT, Perplexity and Claude can read the site, and whether it has an llms.txt.
  • Measuring enquiriesGoogle Analytics or Tag Manager, and forms and phone links that can be counted.
  • Reviews and proofReviews, ratings or testimonials on the page.
Questions

Questions about the check.

Is the Website Check free?

Yes. It is free for small and medium businesses and needs no sign-in. You can run up to five checks a day.

What does it look at?

The public page you enter: its speed on a phone and a computer, how it shows on a phone, whether it says what you do, where and how to get in touch, your contact details, page titles and structured data, https, robots.txt and sitemap, whether AI assistants can read it, whether Google Analytics or Tag Manager is there, and whether it shows reviews.

Do you change anything on my website?

No. The check only reads pages anyone can see. It never signs in and never changes anything.

What happens to the address I enter?

The report is saved for 30 days so its link works, then deleted. Your IP address is not stored; a scrambled version is used only to count checks per day.

Does it use AI?

No. Every result comes from fixed, written rules, so the same page always gets the same answer.

Why check only one page?

Most visitors land on the homepage first, so that is where most of the wins are. Enter another address to check a different page.
