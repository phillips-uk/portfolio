Questions

Questions about websites.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on what's involved. We quote after the free call, in writing, before anything starts, and the call puts you under no obligation.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size of the site and how quickly the content comes together. The plan sets out the timings before work starts.

Can I update it myself?

If you sell online we usually build on Shopify, where you manage products and orders yourself. For other sites, the plan sets out who makes changes and how.

Will it show up on Google?

It will be built so Google can read and index it properly from day one. Where it ranks depends on competition and on local SEO work over time: see local SEO.

Do you work with businesses outside Ely?

Yes. We're based just outside Ely and work with businesses across Cambridgeshire and further afield. Most of the work happens by email and video call.
