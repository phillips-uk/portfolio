Questions about websites.

Why choose you over another web designer?

Because I've run a business from its website. I built The Pommier on Shopify in 2015 and grew it into a business selling £900k a year with two retail outlets in central London, then built the websites for Lee Renée and Myia Bonner, so I plan a site around enquiries, follow-up and repeat business, not just how it looks. You also get search, ads, tracking and email from one person.

How much does a website cost?

Websites start at £995, depending on what you need. That includes a tool for your customers, such as a price calculator. Technical maintenance and security cost £45 a month. We can talk through SEO, Google Ads and any other work you'd like to add.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size of the site and how quickly the content comes together. The plan sets out the timings before work starts.

Can I update it myself?

If you sell online I usually build on Shopify, where you manage products and orders yourself. For other sites, the plan sets out who makes changes and how.

Will it show up on Google?

It will be built so Google can read and index it properly from day one. Where it ranks depends on competition and on local SEO work over time: see local SEO.
