Questions

Questions about websites.

Why choose you over another web designer?

Because I've run a business from its website. I built The Pommier on Shopify in 2015 and grew it to more than 240 designers, a shop in Covent Garden and customers around the world, so I plan a site around enquiries, follow-up and repeat business, not just how it looks. You also get search, ads, tracking and email from one person.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on what's involved. Once I know what you need, I quote in writing before anything starts. Asking puts you under no obligation.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size of the site and how quickly the content comes together. The plan sets out the timings before work starts.

Can I update it myself?

If you sell online I usually build on Shopify, where you manage products and orders yourself. For other sites, the plan sets out who makes changes and how.

Will it show up on Google?

It will be built so Google can read and index it properly from day one. Where it ranks depends on competition and on local SEO work over time: see local SEO.

Can you work with whoever does our marketing now?

Yes. I can take the work on myself, or work alongside the person or team who does it now, helping with the strategy, the execution or both.

Do you work with businesses outside Ely?

Yes. I'm based in Ely and work with businesses across Cambridgeshire and further afield. Most of the work happens by email and video call.
