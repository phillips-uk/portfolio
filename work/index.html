Client · SEO

ScotFuel

SEO and tracking · Started November 2026

ScotFuel compares home heating oil prices across Scotland and lets neighbours order together. The challenge: competing with national price comparison sites for searches like ‘heating oil Pitlochry’, when 68% of Scotland’s oil-heated homes are outside towns and villages with 500 or more people (Scotland’s Census 2022).

What I've done so far
  • Each town page covers the surrounding postcode district as well as the town, using local census figures and ScotFuel’s own supplier prices.
  • A Scotland page, 20 council area pages and the first 49 town pages, live since 19 November 2026.
  • Four free tools: a heating oil cost calculator, how long your oil will last, tank capacity in litres and Scotland’s oil tank rules.
  • The sitemap, robots.txt, structured data, Google Analytics, Search Console, a cookie banner and privacy wording.
  • Next: more towns in stages once the first pages are indexed.
See it live: scotfuel.com →
Client · Website

Monument Corporate Finance

Website, tools and local SEO · In development

Monument is an Ely-based corporate finance adviser specialising in asset-based lending and trade finance, working across London and Cambridgeshire. The challenge: people looking for these services rarely include a town name in their search, so the site has to be found for the services themselves.

What I've done so far
  • A website that puts the enquiry form at the top.
  • Free tools for finance directors, led by a debtor days calculator that uses GOV.UK payment data to compare a business with its industry.
  • A cash conversion cycle calculator and an asset-based lending estimate.
  • A local SEO plan for London and Cambridgeshire.
  • The site isn’t live yet.
Client · App

A booking app for a barbershop

App design and build · Prototype

The challenge: most booking apps assume the barbers work for the shop. Here, each barber works for themselves, setting their own hours and prices and managing their own clients.

What I've done so far
  • Customers book with a barber based on when that barber is free, and pay the barber directly.
  • Each barber sets their own working days, hours and services.
  • Loyalty cards for each barber, a waitlist, walk-ins and ratings after a cut.
  • Barber profiles with photos of their work and links to Instagram and TikTok.
  • The shop owner can see the week’s bookings for every chair and post events.
  • Customers can book from the shop’s mobile website and install the app on their phone.

Geo-Bucketing Bid Model

tCPA Modelling · Location Value · B2C Acquisition

Replaced flat national CPA targets with location-based bidding tiers built from demand, conversion rate and margin data. Allowed high-value areas to be pushed more aggressively while lower-value areas were managed conservatively. Delivered 12% cost reduction, ~16% margin improvement and 19% growth in high-value location conversions.
