Questions

Questions about Google Ads.

How much should I spend on Google Ads?

Enough to get a clear signal, and no more than the returns justify. I look at what a customer is worth to you and what clicks cost in your area, then suggest a starting budget in the plan.

How much do you charge?

It depends on what's involved. Once I know what you need, I quote in writing before anything starts. Asking puts you under no obligation.

Who owns the account?

You do. It stays in your name; I work in it through my manager account, and you can remove that access at any time.

How quickly will I see results?

Ads can bring enquiries from the first day, but an account needs a few weeks of data to settle before it can be improved with confidence.

Can you fix an account someone else set up?

Yes. I start by reviewing what is there, then rebuild what needs it and keep what works.

Can you work with whoever runs our ads now?

Yes. I can take the work on myself, or work alongside the person or team who does it now, helping with the strategy, the execution or both.
