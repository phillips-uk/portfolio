How long does local SEO take?

Usually months rather than weeks. Some fixes, like a complete Business Profile, can show quickly; pages, listings and reviews build over time. The monthly report shows the trend.

What are AEO and GEO?

Answer engine optimisation (AEO) is making your pages give clear, direct answers that search engines can show as the answer. Generative engine optimisation (GEO) is making your business easy for AI tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Google's AI Overviews to understand, trust and mention. Both build on good local SEO rather than replacing it.

Can you get my business recommended by ChatGPT?

Nobody can promise that. What I can do is make the facts about your business clear and consistent on your site, your Business Profile and the listings and review sites that matter, make sure your pages answer the questions customers ask, and check regularly what the AI assistants say.

Do I need a Google Business Profile?

If you serve customers locally, yes: it is what appears in the map results. If you visit customers rather than have premises, it can be set up without showing your address.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what's involved. Once I know what you need, I quote in writing before anything starts. Asking puts you under no obligation.

Can you work with whoever does our marketing now?

Yes. I can take the work on myself, or work alongside the person or team who does it now, helping with the strategy, the execution or both.

Do you work outside Ely?

Yes. I'm based in Ely and work with businesses across Cambridgeshire and further afield. Most of the work happens by email and video call.
