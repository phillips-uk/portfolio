Questions

Questions about local SEO.

How long does local SEO take?

Usually months rather than weeks. Some fixes, like a complete Business Profile, can show quickly; pages, listings and reviews build over time. The monthly report shows the trend.

Do I need a Google Business Profile?

If you serve customers locally, yes: it is what appears in the map results. If you visit customers rather than have premises, it can be set up without showing your address.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what's involved. I quote after the free call, in writing, before anything starts, and the call puts you under no obligation.

Can you guarantee first place on Google?

No, and you should be wary of anyone who does. I can tell you what I'll do, why, and show you the results each month.

Can you work with whoever does our marketing now?

Yes. I can take the work on myself, or work alongside the person or team who does it now, helping with the strategy, the execution or both.

Do you work outside Ely?

Yes. I'm based just outside Ely and work with businesses across Cambridgeshire and further afield. Most of the work happens by email and video call.
