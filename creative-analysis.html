Hook quality 8 Close-up motion in the opening frame creates an immediate pattern interrupt. The on-screen text caption loads within the first second, giving the viewer a clear reason to stay.
Visual hierarchy 7 Subject occupies the upper two-thirds of the frame with negative space used cleanly below. Reading order is intuitive. Minor distraction from a competing background element in the bottom-right corner.

Priority fix: add a subtle branded element to the bottom-left corner.
HOW IT WORKS

Five dimensions. One composite score.

Hook quality
Does the first frame stop the scroll? Assessed on pattern interrupt, emotional trigger, and immediate visual impact.
Visual hierarchy
Is there a clear focal point? The reading order should be obvious without effort.
Copy clarity
Can the message be understood in under two seconds? Penalises ambiguous headlines and missing CTAs.
Brand consistency
Coherent colours, fonts, and tone. A creative that could belong to any brand scores low.
Fatigue signals
Absence of overused formats: plain white background product shots, talking-head UGC with no hook variant, generic CTA text. High score means low fatigue risk.

Each dimension scored 1–10. Composite is the equal-weighted average. Weak signals flagged on any dimension scoring 6 or below.

GOOD TO KNOW

Practical details

Each creative costs roughly £0.03–0.06 to score (claude-opus-4-7 pricing). A batch of ten images runs to about £0.50.
Videos are supported. The tool extracts three frames — opening, mid, and close — and scores each one separately. Requires a local install.
This tool is for analysing existing creatives, not generating new ones. It tells you what is weak and why. What to do about it is a separate conversation.
