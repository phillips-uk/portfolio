Questions

Questions about marketing systems.

Which CRM do you use?

Whichever suits you. If you already have one I work with it; if not, I suggest one that fits the size of the business and the budget.

Is this worth it for a small business?

Often a few simple pieces are enough: tracking that works and one place for enquiries. The plan only includes what earns its keep.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what's involved. Once I know what you need, I quote in writing before anything starts. Asking puts you under no obligation.

Will you show my team how it works?

Yes. Each system is handed over with a walkthrough and notes.

Can you work with whoever does our marketing now?

Yes. I can take the work on myself, or work alongside the person or team who does it now, helping with the strategy, the execution or both.

Do you work outside Ely?

Yes. I'm based in Ely and work with businesses across Cambridgeshire and further afield. Most of the work happens by email and video call.
