Google Ads Check

A health check of your Google Ads account, using the same checks I'd run by hand.

What it does

Sign in with Google and choose your account, or use your Google Ads customer ID. The check looks at your settings and the last 30 days of results. It puts the most important fixes first.

How it helps

If the check spots wasted spend, it estimates what that could cost over the next 30 days. Use the estimate to decide what to fix first, whether you do it yourself or ask me.

What it looks at
  • How Google Ads counts enquiries and sales
  • Settings that quietly waste budget, such as location targeting
  • Searches that cost money but led to no recorded enquiries or sales in the last 30 days
  • Keywords, ads, bidding and budgets
  • Shopping and Performance Max, if you run them

Website Check

A check of the public page you enter, from how fast it loads to how easy it is to get in touch.

What it does

Enter a web address. The check uses Google's speed test and gives you a report on what's working, what to fix and where to start.

How it helps

See how well your page works on a phone and how easy it is to find your contact details. Use the report to decide what to fix before you spend more on attracting visitors.

What it looks at
  • Speed on a phone and a computer
  • Whether it says what you do, where, and how to get in touch
  • Your contact details and your Google Business Profile link
  • Page titles and descriptions, and whether the site lets AI assistants read the page
  • Signs that enquiry tracking is set up, and whether the page shows reviews
