Privacy notice

What this website collects about you, why, who it goes to and how long it is kept. Last updated 15 November 2026.

The company is the controller of the personal data described here. For anything about your data, email contact@phillips-uk.com.

When you ask me a question

The "Ask me a question" form collects your name, your email address, your business or website if you add it, your question, the page you sent it from and the country your connection comes from. I use these only to reply to you and to keep track of the conversation, including any work that follows. The lawful basis is my legitimate interest in answering questions people send me, and taking steps you ask for before any contract.

Your question is stored on Cloudflare, emailed to me through Resend, and my reply comes from Google Workspace email. I keep it for as long as it is useful for the conversation and any work that follows, then delete it.

When you sign up to The Monday Brief

The sign-up form collects your email address. I also record when and on which page you signed up, the wording you agreed to, and when you confirmed. Nobody is added to the list until they click the link in the confirmation email. The lawful basis is your consent.

The list and the emails are handled by Resend; the sign-up records are stored on Cloudflare. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing takes effect straight away. Sign-ups that are never confirmed are deleted after 7 days. If you unsubscribe, I keep your address only on a do-not-email list, so you aren't emailed again. You can sign up again at any time.

Stopping spam

Both forms limit how many times they can be used from one connection in an hour. To do that they store a scrambled, one-way form of your connection's address. The address itself is not stored.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics, through Google Tag Manager, to see which pages people visit and which forms they use. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects details such as the pages you view, your device and browser, and your approximate location. Google provides the service. You can stop it in your browser settings or with Google's opt-out add-on.

Hosting

Cloudflare hosts this website. It processes technical details of each visit, such as your IP address, to deliver the pages and protect the site.

Who else sees your data

Only the providers named above, each acting for me: Cloudflare (hosting and storage), Resend (email) and Google (email and analytics). Some of them are based outside the UK, including in the United States. I don't sell your data or share it with anyone else for their own marketing.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the data I hold about you, ask me to correct or delete it, object to how I use it, or withdraw your consent at any time. Email contact@phillips-uk.com. If you're unhappy with how I've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
